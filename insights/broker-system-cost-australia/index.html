Build, buy or connect: what a broker system costs in Australia

Most brokerages do not need a new broking system. They need the systems they have to work together. Here is what each path costs.

By Anastas Manojlovski · 26 November 2026

Contents

  1. The question behind the question
  2. Three paths
  3. What it costs with us
  4. What moves the price
  5. How to turn a range into a fixed price
  6. Questions brokers ask

Short answer. Most owner-led brokerages do not need a new broking system. They need the systems they already have to work together. Connecting those systems usually costs $20k–45k for a first version, built in four to six weeks. A full custom build costs more and takes longer. Your final estimate comes after a discovery session.

The question behind the question

When brokers ask us what a system costs, they usually describe the same problem. The broking system, the CRM, Xero and Outlook do not talk to each other. Staff type the same data twice. Month-end journals are done by hand. Certificates of currency and endorsements wait for a person.

So the real question is not “what does software cost?” It is “which of three paths fixes this, and what does each one cost?”

Three paths

1. Buy and configure

You keep or change your broking system (for example WinBEAT or Steadfast Insight) and use its own features and add-ons.

  • Cost: a licence per user each month, plus set-up and data migration. Most vendors do not publish their prices, so ask for a quote that includes migration and training.
  • Good for: standard broking work, when the vendor’s workflow fits how you work.
  • Watch for: the gaps between systems stay. The licence cost grows with every new staff member.

2. Connect and build around your broking system

You keep your broking system. We connect it to your CRM, Xero and email, and build the missing parts on top: a client portal, renewal workflows, document handling, AI on your own data.

  • Cost: see the table below. This is where most of our broker work sits.
  • Good for: brokerages that are happy with their broking system, but lose time between systems.
  • Watch for: access to the broking system’s data. Some systems give a full API, some give exports only, and some limit access. We check this in the discovery session, before you commit.

3. Full custom build

You build your own system, or a large part of one, for example a claims platform.

  • Cost: see the table below. General Australian guides put large custom platforms at several hundred thousand dollars.
  • Good for: broker networks and firms whose workflow is their advantage, or who must stop depending on a third-party platform.
  • Watch for: you also own the support, the security and the updates. Budget for it every year.

What moves the price

  1. Access to your data. A broking system with a good API costs less to connect than one that gives exports only.
  2. The number of systems. Each extra system (CRM, accounting, email, insurer portals) adds work.
  3. The state of your data. Duplicate clients and missing fields must be fixed before automation works.
  4. Compliance and security. Audit logs, access control and where data is stored add work, and insurers and clients now ask about them.
  5. How clear the requirements are. This is the biggest driver. Unclear requirements make every estimate wide.

How to turn a range into a fixed price

  1. Discovery session. We learn your systems, your data and the problem. You get a short plan and a fixed estimate.
  2. Clickable prototype. Within two weeks, you see the main screens and test them with your team. If you go ahead, the prototype cost is credited against the build.
  3. First version. We build it around your broking system, and show it in a demo before each invoice.

Questions brokers ask

Do we need to replace our broking system?

Usually not. Most brokerages get more value when we connect the system they already use and build what is missing around it.

Why is your range lower than the general guides?

General guides price a full product from scratch with a local agency team. We usually build around your broking system, so there is less to build. Our team is Australian-led, with developers working Australian business hours.

What is not in the price?

The discovery session, hosting and licences for other software, and support after go-live. We tell you each of these in the estimate.

How long until we see something?

A clickable prototype within two weeks. A first version in four to six weeks.
