Privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

This policy tells you how Beyond Zenith Software Pty Ltd (ABN 54 654 970 091) (“we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects your personal information. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

  • When you use the contact form: your name, your email address, your company name (if you give it), how you heard about us (if you tell us) and your message.
  • When you email us: your email address, your name and the content of your email.
  • When you visit the website: our hosting provider records technical data, such as your IP address, your browser type and the pages you open. This data helps to keep the website secure and working.
  • Website analytics: we use Ahrefs Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful. It records the page you open, the website that sent you, your approximate location (country and city), your language, your screen size, and your browser and device type. It also counts clicks on links and form submissions. It uses your IP address to find your location, but it does not store the IP address. It does not use cookies, and it does not track you from one day to the next or across other websites. We see only totals, not individual visitors.

We do not use cookies or advertising trackers on this website. The website loads its fonts and images from our own domain. The only script from a third party is the Ahrefs Web Analytics script.

How we use it

We use your personal information to:

  • reply to your enquiry and discuss the work you describe
  • keep a record of our communication with you
  • obey the law.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not add you to a marketing list unless you ask us to.

Who we share it with

We use these service providers. They store or process data for us:

  • Netlify, Inc. (United States) hosts this website and receives the contact form.
  • Google LLC (Google Workspace) provides our email. Google can store data in the United States and in other countries.
  • Ahrefs Pte Ltd (Singapore) provides our website analytics. Ahrefs can store data in the United States.

Thus, your personal information can go to and be stored in countries outside Australia. We use only providers that protect personal information with industry-standard security. We do not give your personal information to other organisations unless you agree or the law requires it.

How we protect it and how long we keep it

We protect your personal information with password-protected accounts and encrypted connections. We keep it only for as long as we need it for the purposes above, or for as long as the law requires. Then we delete it.

Access and correction

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information that we hold about you. You can also ask us to correct it or delete it. Send an email to hello@beyondzenith.com.au. We reply within 30 days.

Complaints

If you think that we did not handle your personal information correctly, send an email to hello@beyondzenith.com.au. We will examine your complaint and reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you can make a complaint to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

We can change this policy. When we do, we publish the new version on this page and change the date at the top.
