Software and data for insurance brokers

Broking runs on client data that sits in many systems. We help brokers join that data, build on it, and get it ready for AI.

Start a conversationEmail hello@beyondzenith.com.au

What we see

  • Client records in many systems

    The same client is in the CRM, the broking platform and the policy systems, often with different details in each.

  • Work done twice

    People copy data from one system to another by hand, and reports from different systems do not agree.

  • Hard questions from outside

    Insurers, auditors and enterprise clients ask how you protect client data. Some ask for certifications such as SOC 2.

  • AI that needs clean data

    AI tools give poor answers when the client data under them is incomplete or in conflict.

What we build

  • One record for each client

    We match client records across your CRM, broking platform and policy systems, so each client has one record that every system can use.

  • A client portal on your broking system

    Clients see their policies, documents and renewal dates. We read the data from your broking system. You do not replace it.

  • Renewal and document workflows

    We read schedules, certificates and emails, and put the data where your team needs it. A person checks each result before it goes to a client.

  • AI on your own data

    Search and summaries over your client files, in your own cloud account. We measure accuracy on your own records before go-live.

  • Answers for security reviews

    Access control, audit logs and data location, written down so you can answer insurers, auditors and clients.

How a project runs

  1. Discovery session

    We learn your systems, your data and the problem. You get a short plan and a fixed estimate.

  2. Clickable prototype

    Within two weeks, you see the main screens and test them with your team.

  3. First version

    In four to six weeks, we build the first version around your broking system.

  4. Run and improve

    A team sized to your budget supports the system and adds features each month.

Questions brokers ask

  • Do we need to replace our broking system?

    Usually not. Most brokerages get more value when we build around the system they already use. We read its data, join it with your other systems, and add what is missing.

  • Where does our client data stay?

    In Australia. We build on Amazon Web Services in the Sydney Region. When your data must stay in your own AWS account, we build in that account.

  • Who does the work?

    Beyond Zenith is Australian-led, with developers working Australian business hours. One senior lead is your contact from the first workshop to the last release.

  • What does it cost?

    It depends on your systems, your data and how clear the requirements are. The discovery session gives you a fixed estimate before you commit to the build.

  • Can we keep client knowledge when a senior broker leaves?

    Yes, when the knowledge is in your files, emails and notes. We bring it into one client record that the next broker can search.

Our work

For an Australian insurance broker, we are building one source of truth for client identity.

It matches client records across Salesforce and the broker’s other systems, so that each client has one record that every system can use. It is also the base for the broker’s AI work. For the same broker, we scoped what SOC 2 certification for one of its products would take.
