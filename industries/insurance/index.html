Software and data for insurance brokers

Broking runs on client data that sits in many systems. We help brokers join that data, build on it, and get it ready for AI.

Start a conversationEmail anastas@beyondzenith.com.au

What we see

  • Client records in many systems

    The same client is in the CRM, the broking platform and the policy systems, often with different details in each.

  • Work done twice

    People copy data from one system to another by hand, and reports from different systems do not agree.

  • Hard questions from outside

    Insurers, auditors and enterprise clients ask how you protect client data. Some ask for certifications such as SOC 2.

  • AI that needs clean data

    AI tools give poor answers when the client data under them is incomplete or in conflict.

Our work

For an Australian insurance broker, we are building one source of truth for client identity.

It matches client records across Salesforce and the broker’s other systems, so that each client has one record that every system can use. It is also the base for the broker’s AI work. For the same broker, we scoped what SOC 2 certification for one of its products would take.
