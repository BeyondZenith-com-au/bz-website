Software for freight and logistics

Freight platforms must run every day, and a bad release costs real money. We build and maintain freight software, and we release changes with care.

Start a conversationEmail anastas@beyondzenith.com.au

What we see

  • A platform that must not stop

    Carriers and shippers use the platform every day. A failed release or lost data stops their work, not only yours.

  • Cloud set-ups that grew fast

    Many platforms grew faster than their cloud security. Old settings and wide access stay in place until someone reviews them.

  • Manual onboarding

    Each new customer needs rates, lanes and accounts set up, and people often do this by hand.

  • Pricing and quoting

    Quotes depend on many rates and rules. AI can help, but only on a platform that is stable first.

Our work

For an Australian freight technology platform, our delivery team maintains and develops the product each month.

We put in a stricter release process: a risk rating for each change, peer review, AI code review, a test merge in a sandbox, and a final check by the tech lead. We also reviewed the platform’s AWS security, and we are fixing the findings in order of risk before the next work on onboarding and AI.
