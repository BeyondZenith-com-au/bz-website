  • We have a prototype

How we work with AI

  • The best model for the job

    We choose the model that suits your task, your data and your budget, and we test it on your own records.

  • Accuracy measured on your data

    Before go-live, we measure how often the output is correct on records from your own business.

  • Your data in your account

    When your data must stay in your own AWS account, we use Claude on Amazon Bedrock in that account.
