Software delivery teams for Australian businesses

A dedicated team that builds and maintains your product. A senior lead in Australia runs it, and you can make it larger or smaller as the work changes. You see working software before each invoice. Based in Melbourne.

Start a conversationEmail hello@beyondzenith.com.au

What you get

  • One senior lead

    One senior person leads your engagement from the first workshop to the last release. That person is your contact for priorities, decisions and problems.

  • Local leadership, global talent

    Your lead is in Australia. The engineers come from our delivery teams across Australia and Asia, so you get the skills that the work needs.

  • A capped monthly budget

    You agree a monthly budget before the work starts. You always know the maximum cost of a month.

  • No lock-in

    Make the team larger or smaller as your work changes. You do not sign up for a long fixed contract.

  • A demo before every invoice

    Each month, we show you working software in a demo. Then we send the invoice for that month.

  • Code and data you own

    The code and the data are yours. Nothing is hidden from you, and you can take everything with you.

How it works

  1. Discover

    Workshops to map your systems, your data and the problem that is worth solving first.

  2. Plan

    A costed plan with monthly milestones, so you know what you approve.

  3. Build

    Working software every month, shown in a demo before we send the invoice.

  4. Run

    Support, monitoring and improvements after go-live. The code and data stay yours.

When a delivery team is a good fit

You have a product to build or to keep improving, and you do not want to hire and manage a full engineering team yourself.

A delivery team also suits a business with an existing product that needs more people for a period: a new feature set, a platform change, or work that your own team does not have time for.

Why Beyond Zenith

Our founder, Anastas Manojlovski, has led delivery teams of more than 25 engineers across Australia and Asia.

Before Beyond Zenith, Anastas led solution architecture across Australia and New Zealand for nine years at Whispir, on systems for emergency services, health departments and councils that had to work on the worst day of the year. Since 2021, Anastas has built and led engineering teams that deliver cloud, data and AI platforms for Australian businesses.
