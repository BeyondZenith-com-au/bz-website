Technical advisory before you commit to a large build

Architecture, delivery planning and an honest second opinion. Discovery has a fixed price, you keep the written plan, and if we then build it, we credit the fee.

Start a conversationEmail anastas@beyondzenith.com.au

What you get

  • Fixed-price discovery

    You know the cost of the discovery before it starts.

  • A written plan you keep

    You get the architecture and a costed plan with monthly milestones in writing. The plan is yours, whoever builds it.

  • An honest second opinion

    We tell you what we think of a plan, an architecture or a build that is already in progress, including the parts that you may not want to hear.

  • Fee credited if we build it

    If you then choose us to build it, we credit the discovery fee against the build.

How it works

  1. First conversation

    You tell us about the problem, the systems you have and the decision in front of you. We tell you if discovery is the right next step.

  2. Discovery

    Workshops with your team to map your systems, your data and the problem that is worth solving first.

  3. Written plan

    The architecture, the risks, and a costed plan with monthly milestones, in writing.

  4. Your decision

    Use the plan with your own team, with another supplier, or with us. If you choose us, we credit the discovery fee.

When advisory is a good fit

You are about to approve a large build and you want an independent view first.

Advisory also helps when a project is late or over budget and you want to know why, or when you must choose between suppliers, platforms or architectures.

Why Beyond Zenith

Our founder, Anastas Manojlovski, led solution architecture across Australia and New Zealand for nine years at Whispir.

That work was with emergency services, health departments and councils, on systems that had to work on the worst day of the year. Since 2024, Anastas has built and led engineering teams that deliver cloud, data and AI platforms for Australian businesses.
