Data and integration platforms for Australian businesses

We connect the systems you already run, so your data is in one place that you own and can use. The platform is built on AWS, as code, and is ready for AI from day one. Based in Melbourne.

Start a conversationEmail hello@beyondzenith.com.au

What you get

  • Your systems connected

    We connect the systems that your business already uses, so data moves between them and people stop copying it by hand.

  • One view across every system

    Your data is in one place, so your reports and decisions use the same numbers.

  • Built on AWS, as code

    The platform runs on AWS, and every part of it is defined as code. You can see it, review it and build it again.

  • Yours to keep

    The platform, its code and your data are yours. Nothing is hidden from you, and you can take everything with you.

  • Ready for AI from day one

    Your data is clean and in one place, so you can use it with AI tools when you are ready.

  • Support after go-live

    We monitor the platform, fix problems and add improvements. We do not leave after the launch.

How it works

  1. Discover

    Workshops to map your systems, your data and the problem that is worth solving first.

  2. Plan

    A costed plan with monthly milestones, so you know what you approve.

  3. Build

    Working software every month, shown in a demo before we send the invoice.

  4. Run

    Support, monitoring and improvements after go-live. The code and data stay yours.

When a data platform is a good fit

Your data is in many systems, and nobody can see all of it in one place.

Typical signs: people export spreadsheets to combine data by hand, different reports give different numbers, or you want to use AI but your data is not ready for it.

Why Beyond Zenith
