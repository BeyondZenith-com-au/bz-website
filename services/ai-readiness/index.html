An AI readiness review before you roll out Claude

A fixed-price review of your work, your data and your security. You get a written report that tells you where AI can help first, what must change before you start, and what the first step costs. If we then do the work, we waive the fee.

Start a conversationEmail anastas@beyondzenith.com.au

What you get

  • A fixed price

    You know the cost of the review before it starts.

  • Use cases in order of value

    We find the tasks where AI can save your people the most time. We put them in order of value and effort, so you know where to start.

  • A check of your data

    We find where your data is, who can see it, and what must change before an AI tool can use it safely.

  • Security and privacy risks

    We compare each use case with your security standards and with the Australian Privacy Principles. We tell you which risks to fix first.

  • A written report you keep

    You get the use cases, the risks and a costed plan for a first pilot, in writing. The report is yours, whoever does the work.

  • Fee waived if we continue

    If you then choose us to do the work in the report, we waive the fee for the review.

How it works

  1. First conversation

    You tell us how your teams work now and what you want AI to do. We tell you if the review is the right next step.

  2. Workshop

    A workshop with the people who do the work. We map their tasks, their systems and the data they use.

  3. Review

    With your permission, we examine your data, your access controls and your security settings. This work stays inside your own environment.

  4. Written report

    The use cases in order of value, the risks, and a costed plan for a first pilot. Then the decision is yours.

When a review is a good fit

You want to use Claude or other AI tools, but you do not know where to start, or you are not sure that your data is safe to use.

A review also helps when your people already use AI tools without a policy, or when a first pilot did not give the results that you expected.
