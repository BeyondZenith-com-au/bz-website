Frequently asked questions

Short answers about how we work, what it costs, who owns the work and how we keep your data safe. If your question is not here, send us an email.

Start a conversationEmail hello@beyondzenith.com.au

The company

  • Who is in the Beyond Zenith team?

    We are a small core team in Melbourne with a bench of onshore and offshore engineers that we know and trust. Our roles include software developers, senior software developers, DevOps engineers, technical leads, solution architects, data engineers, data scientists, QA engineers, UI/UX designers, project managers and a virtual CTO. We choose the people for each project, manage them and stay accountable for their work. The team gets bigger or smaller as the project needs. We use onshore engineers, offshore engineers or a mix, based on your budget, scope and requirements.

  • Where do you work?

    We work with clients across Australia and New Zealand, and we can work with clients in other countries. Our core team is in Melbourne and works in Australian time zones.

  • What size of company do you work with?

    We work with start-ups, scale-ups and mid-sized businesses. Our clients usually need senior engineering and AI skills but do not want to hire a full team, or they need to deliver faster than their own team can.

  • Which industries do you work in?

    We work mostly in regulated industries: insurance, financial services, payments, freight and logistics, government and health. We know how to deliver where security, compliance and reliability matter. We also work in other industries.

  • Why choose Beyond Zenith and not a big consultancy or a freelancer?

    A big consultancy gives you a large team, high rates and a long ramp-up. A freelancer gives you one person and no back-up. We are in the middle. You work directly with senior people who do the work, and we can add engineers when the project needs them. We work as part of your team, in your tools and your cloud. We change the scope and the team as your needs change. After go-live, we stay accountable for what we built.

Price and contracts

  • How do you charge?

    We offer fixed-price projects, monthly retainers or a mix of the two. First, we help you define the outcome and the scope. Then we agree the model that suits your budget and how you like to work.

  • What is the smallest engagement?

    Our smallest engagements start from $5,000 AUD (excluding GST).

  • How fast can you start?

    For most projects, we can start within days of agreement. Larger teams can take longer to put together.

  • What happens after go-live?

    We give a warranty period of 30 or 90 days on the work we deliver, depending on the project. We also offer ongoing support. You can choose a support plan that suits you, from on-call fixes to a monthly retainer.

  • Who owns the code and the intellectual property?

    You do. When the invoices are paid, you own all the code, documents and other work from the engagement. We keep nothing.

AI, cloud and data

  • Which AI models do you use?

    We help you choose the right model and platform for the job. We can work with any major AI platform. We usually recommend Anthropic’s Claude or OpenAI’s models, because they are the strongest for business work today.

  • Can our data stay in Australia?

    Yes. When your data must stay in Australia, we build in the AWS Sydney and Melbourne regions and use Claude through Amazon Bedrock in those regions. Your data stays in your own AWS account, and AWS does not use it to train AI models.

  • Which cloud and data platforms do you work with?

    AWS is our first choice. We also work with Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, Databricks and Snowflake.

Teams and advisory

  • How big is a delivery team?

    A delivery team usually starts with a technical lead and a solution architect, who scope and design the solution. Then we add engineers based on the scope, the budget and the agreed outcome. Most teams have between 3 and 10 people, and some have more.

  • Do you work in our tools and systems?

    Yes. We prefer to work in your environment: your cloud accounts, code repositories, ticket system and chat tools. Then you can see the work every day, and everything stays with you at the end.

  • What does technical advisory look like?

    It depends on what you need. We offer a fixed-scope review, regular advice from 1 day per month, or a part-time virtual CTO.

Security and compliance

  • Are you ISO 27001 or SOC 2 certified?

    We do not hold these certifications ourselves. We build to the standards that our clients must meet, such as ISO 27001, SOC 2 and APRA CPS 234, and we work inside your security controls. We can also help you prepare for an audit or a certification.

  • Do you sign NDAs and do background checks?

    Yes. We sign NDAs, and we can arrange police checks for the people on your project. When a project needs a government security clearance, we work with you on the clearance process.
