Your data stays in your environment

We work inside your own systems and cloud accounts, with access that you give and can remove. We build to the security standards that you must meet. The code and the data stay yours.

Start a conversationEmail anastas@beyondzenith.com.au

What this means for you

  • Your accounts, your control

    We work in your cloud accounts and your tools. You give us access, and you can remove it at any time.

  • Your data stays where it is

    We work with your data in your own systems. We do not copy it to systems of ours.

  • Built to your standards

    If you must meet ISO 27001, SOC 2 or your own policies, we build and document the work to those controls.

  • Code you own

    The code is in your repositories, and the infrastructure is defined as code. You can see every change.

  • AI that stays in your account

    When your data must stay in your own AWS account, we use Claude on Amazon Bedrock in that account.

  • One accountable lead

    A senior lead in Australia is accountable for the team, the work and the access that the team uses.

How we manage access

  1. Agree the access

    Before the work starts, we agree which systems we need, what level of access, and why. We ask for no more than the work needs.

  2. Work in your environment

    We use the accounts that you give us, under your policies and your logs.

  3. Show every change

    Changes go into your repositories, and each month we show you working software in a demo.

  4. Hand back

    When the work ends, you remove our access. You keep the code, the infrastructure definitions and the documents.

Questions your risk team will ask

Does Beyond Zenith hold ISO 27001 or SOC 2 certification?

No. We work inside your environment and build to the controls that you must meet. Your own policies and audits apply to the work, and we give you the documents that your auditor asks for.

Where is our data?

Your data stays in your own systems and cloud accounts. We work with it there, and we do not copy it to systems of ours.

Who on your team can access our systems?

Only the people who work on your engagement, with the access that you approve. Our senior lead in Australia is accountable for their work and their access.

Can we use AI with our data and keep it in our own account?

Yes. When your data must stay in your own AWS account, we use Claude on Amazon Bedrock in that account.

Who owns the code?

You do. The code is in your repositories and the infrastructure is defined as code, so you can see it, review it and build it again.

How do we report a security problem?

Email anastas@beyondzenith.com.au. Our security.txt file gives the same contact.
